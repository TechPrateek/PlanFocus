FocusFlow AI

An adaptive, stress-free smart study planner and focus companion for college students.

The Unique Kernel

Realistic, Adaptive Rebalancing: Unlike rigid to-do lists or unrealistic calendars that fail when you fall behind, FocusFlow AI provides realistic time-budgeted schedules. If you miss a study session, one click intelligently reorganizes remaining days based on exam urgency and topic difficulty—no guilt, no broken schedules.

The Core Loop

1
Quick Setup Input subjects, topics, exam dates, difficulty levels, and available daily study hours.
2
Intelligent Scheduling Generates a balanced daily roadmap prioritized by upcoming exams and topic weights.
3
Integrated Focus Session Launch the built-in Pomodoro timer right on the scheduled topic with audio chimes.
4
Complete or Adaptive Rebalance Mark sessions complete to build streaks, or click "Rebalance Plan" if life happens.

Project Scope Boundary

  • NOW Quick Setup Wizard: Subjects, topics, difficulty, exam dates, daily hours.
  • NOW Adaptive Scheduler: Priority-weighted time-block allocation engine.
  • NOW Today's Focus Dashboard: Clean Linear/Notion layout with next actions.
  • NOW Interactive Pomodoro Timer: Work & break intervals with audio feedback.
  • NOW Rebalance Engine: Redistributes missed topics across remaining days.
  • NOW Analytics & Streaks: Hours studied, topics done, and active streak.
  • NOW Local Storage Persistence: Works instantly with zero setup or login.
  • LATER Syllabus PDF Parsing: Auto-extract courses from uploaded files.
  • LATER Google / Outlook Calendar Sync: Two-way calendar integration.
  • LATER Multi-device Cloud Sync: Collaborative study rooms.
  • CUT Social Feeds & Leaderboards: Distracting for deep student focus.
  • CUT Mandatory User Login: Hinders quick evaluation and demo.
  • CUT Grade Predictions: Misleading, unprovable, and unethical.
