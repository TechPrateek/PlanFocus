Feature Specifications & Acceptance Criteria

Deterministic Priority Scheduling Algorithm:

Priority = (DifficultyWeight / (DaysUntilExam + 0.1)) * WorkloadHours
  • Never exceeds user's daily study hours budget (e.g. 3h/day).
  • Never schedules topics after their exam date has passed.
  • Accurately sorts topics by urgent exams and higher difficulty weights (Hard = 2x, Med = 1.5x, Easy = 1x).
  • Allows optional Gemini AI enhancement for study tips while maintaining 100% rule-based offline reliability.

Adaptive Rebalance & Capacity Conflict Warning:

  • Marking a topic as "Missed" flags the deficit immediately without panic.
  • "Rebalance Plan" recalculates remaining topics across future available days without dropping content.
  • Capacity Warning triggers if remaining hours required > remaining days * daily hours limit.
  • Surfaces actionable guidance (e.g., "Consider adding +1.2 hours/day or reviewing high-priority topics first").

Web Audio Focus Timer (Pomodoro):

  • Work (25m), Short Break (5m), Long Break (15m) with custom adjustments.
  • Clean SVG circular progress countdown with monospace digits.
  • Synthesizes Web Audio chimes upon completion; gracefully falls back to browser notifications/visual pulses if sound blocked.
  • Logs completed intervals directly to active topic's study log.

Persistence & Demo Simulation for Judges:

  • 100% localStorage persistence — refresh-proof, zero login required.
  • One-click "Load Sample Student Plan" populates realistic CS & Chemistry courses with upcoming exams.
  • One-click "Simulate Missed Session" demonstrates adaptive rebalance in 5 seconds during video demos.
  • Clean reset button to wipe data with confirmation dialog.
